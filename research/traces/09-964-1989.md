## Glasshouse height (revised while tracing the 993)

The vendor sheet puts the glass sill at 0.861 m and the glass top at 1.175 m. Vertical intensity
profiles through PHOTO01 at x 2.2 / 2.4 m (door-top seal 0.870–0.875 m, glass-top seal ≈ 1.19–1.21 m)
agree with the 993's Porsche studio photo of the same series (0.873 / 1.186 m; the 993 kept the 964
greenhouse). The DLO, quarter glass, window trim, belt and door shoulder were therefore raised by
2.1 px (1.2 cm, `GLASS_SHIFT`); the roof and drip rail (1.217 m, photo 1.219–1.226 m) were already
consistent. Remaining offset: glass top ≈ 1 cm below PHOTO01 at the A-pillar end.

## Discrepancies and uncertainties

- The vendor side view draws the bumpers ≈ 3 cm low, the rear arch lip 2.6 cm low and the rear bumper
  7 cm too deep; PHOTO01, PHOTO05/08, PHOTO20 and the AUTOCAR drawing agree with each other, so the
  lower body follows PHOTO01. The vendor rear view places the exhaust at z 0.52; PHOTO20 gives 0.59
  (photo used).
- Front-lid centre-line profile ahead of the cowl is provisional (not visible in any side view).
- Headlamp x: PHOTO01 0.348 vs COUPE 0.32 (photo used). Lamp Ø 0.21 m (drawing/photo) is larger
  than a 7-in unit; the ring/bowl is probably included.
- Tail lamp band: side view 0.094 m tall vs head-on photo ≈ 0.11 m (camera height); drawing + side
  photo used (0.495–0.589).
- The spoiler is modelled retracted (flush), as parked. The black divider between band and lamps
  (≈ 1.7 cm) is not modelled.
- System limitation found: decal outlines are resampled by arc length to 2 × the larger point count
  of the two stops, which chamfers the corners of 4-point boxes (a V notch where a box meets the
  centre line). Boxes here carry 12–28 points along their edges to avoid it; the decal point budget
  (MAX_POINTS 1024) is respected (690 points for this stop paired with itself).
