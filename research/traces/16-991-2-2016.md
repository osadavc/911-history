## Calibration

Shell calibration: see `15-991-1-2012.md` (official drawing: 2,450 mm = 711 px, ground = hub +
335.3 mm, end views 3.373 mm/px across / 3.357 mm/px up). For this stop:

- ride height: official 1,294 mm vs 1,299 mm measured on the drawing → −5 mm on every height
  (the drawing's car is a Carrera S; 1,294–1,298 mm printed).
- length: 4,499 mm official vs 4,496.8 mm on the drawing → each overhang +1.1 mm.
- Result: L 4.499, FA 0.9935, RA 3.4435 (WB 2.450), lofted height 1.294 m.
- Rear-view details (lettering, plate) are placed from the straight Carrera T rear photo P12
  (EU plate 520 mm = 251 px → 2.07 mm/px), anchored on the lamps' inner top edge (OR y 237).

## Checks

- `check-side.png`: the body is the traced drawing itself (silhouette, arches, DLO, door, handle).
- `check-front.png` / `check-rear.png`: strips, intakes, taillights, reflectors and outlets sit on the
  drawn parts; the plate boxes are the EU plate size (the drawing shows no plate).
- `check-top.png`: plan outline and glass on the vendor 991.2 top view; the grille field covers its
  louvres.
- `check-photo-side.png`: pinhole overlay on P01 (hubs = wheel crests 404/893 and 1462/892 px). The
  photo is a close-range street shot; of 5/7/10 m, 5 m puts roof, tail, nose, DLO and handle on the
  photo. (The photo car has optional 20-inch wheels.)

## Uncertainties

- Same shell as stop 15; ride height from the official figure (±3 mm).
- The four-point brake light and the lid louvre direction are not representable (lamp decals are flat
  fills; decal stripes run across the car) — described, not faked.
- `intake-front-centre`, `reflector-rear`, `vent-rear`, `indicator-side`, `taillight-side` and
  `crest` are slot ids not in TRACING.md's table (shared with stops 13/15/17/18).
