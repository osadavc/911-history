## Differences from 07 (MY1978 SC), each from geometry.json 08 / photos

- **Fog lamps built into the front apron** (the 3.2's identifier): photo 06-front (wing
  width scale 1.556 mm/px, corrected ×0.94 for the lamps standing ~0.6 m ahead of the
  widest wing section) gives z 0.44–0.59 m, y 0.31–0.37 m; the Targa 3-view front view gives
  z 0.425–0.591, y 0.29–0.39. Decal `fog-front` z 0.43–0.59, y 0.31–0.374.
- **Side repeaters** (MY1984) on the front wings ahead of the door, level with the arch top:
  Targa 3-view x 1.326 / y 0.679; photo 02 x 1.288 / y 0.678; 1989 brochure x 1.383 /
  y 0.656 → decal x 1.30–1.355, y 0.66–0.69.
- **Black trim**: anodised-black window frames, drip rail, door handles (rendered with the
  rubber finish because satin *line* decals are drawn as panel gaps by the body shader);
  body-colour headlamp rings.
- **Forged Fuchs** (`fuchs`), black star with polished lip (the 1984/85 cars in photos
  09 / 13 and the 16-in cars in 01 / 29 / 30).
- **"Carrera" script** on the lid (photo 17-rear: centred, 0.256 m wide, centre 0.752 m),
  `badge-model` slot in the top plane as on 03 / 05–07.
- **Two body-colour flag mirrors** (photos 09 and 13 of 1984–85 cars show a passenger
  mirror too); housing as the SC, outer edge 0.934 m (dd930 front view, printed).
- Tracks 1372 / 1380. Single tailpipe on the left (photo 17-rear).

## Known uncertainties

- MY1984 has the black-logo reflector strip (red reflective logo + fog lamps from MY1987);
  the decal uses the black lettering.
- Mirror: MY1984 passenger mirror status not stated in geometry.json 08 (photos of 1984–85
  cars show two).
- Front fog-lamp outline is rectangular (photos), size ±1 cm.
- Shell identical to 07 by documentation (B-282 = B-207 widths and overhangs).
