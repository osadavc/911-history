# 12-996-2-2002 — 911 Carrera Coupé (996.2, MY2002–2004)

Script: `research/traces/12-996-2-2002.py` (reads the 996.1 trace `src/data/cars/11-996-1-1998.json`,
writes `src/data/cars/12-996-2-2002.json`, `research/traces/12-996-2-2002.calib.json`,
`check-lamp.png`, `check-front-photo.png`, `check-rear-photo.png`, `check-side-photo-persp-01.png`).
Run the 11 script first; `PHOTOCHECK=0` skips the side-photo fit.

## Approach

The 996.2 is a facelift of the 996.1 narrow body (geometry.json 12; coordinator brief). The only
996.2 drawing (tbp-68297) is the wide-body Carrera 4S with the Turbo apron, so the body is the
996.1 trace, verified against the 996.2 photos, plus the documented facelift changes, each
measured on the 996.2 photos.

## Checks that the body is unchanged (photo 01, studio side profile, tracing: side)

- Hubs (434.5, 973.7)/(1451.7, 973.6) from rim-edge circle fits (432.9 px/m); loaded hub height
  0.303 m (tyre contact v 1105).
- Perspective fit of the 996.1 loft (photofit, ends constrained): IoU 0.942 at D 6 m, ends within
  1–6 px; roof median −2 mm.
- Arch lips measured with the same radial paint-edge method on the 996.1 (11/01) and 996.2
  (12/01) photos: radii agree within 0.5 cm all round (front 0.36–0.38 m, rear 0.36–0.38 m).
- Glasshouse, door gap, handle, marker and tail lamp land on the photo
  (`check-side-photo-persp-01.png`, `check-side-photo.png`).

## Calibration file

`12-996-2-2002.calib.json` points check_car at the 996.1 drawing views (same body) for side, plan,
front and rear, plus photo 01 (`side_photo`). The facelift details are checked on the 996.2 photos
in `check-front-photo.png` / `check-rear-photo.png` (a close front/rear photo cannot be overlaid
orthographically: roof and bumper are at very different distances).

## Known uncertainties

- The apron's 3D shape is the 996.1's (only the openings changed in the model); the real 996.2
  apron has deeper outer openings and small lips ahead of the front wheels (not modelled).
- The rear brace is a line decal, not a surface change.
- Tailpipe position from a slightly high rear photo (±2 cm).
- Many 996.2 cars (e.g. photo 11) carry optional twin round tips; the standard oval tip is used.
