## Calibration

- **PHOTO01**: rim-lip circles fitted on 180 rays (residual 0.2–0.3 px): (524.06, 627.76) and
  (1321.09, 635.85) → 2.8504 mm/px at the wheel plane, axle 0.316 m. Centre-line tips (nose 213 px,
  tail 1666 px) appear 2.4 % short (k = 0.9757; the 964 studio photo of the same series gives 0.980):
  corrected about the image centre they give L = 4.245 with the front axle 0.940 m behind the nose.
  Centre-line features (roof, screen, deck, tips) are corrected with the same k; wheel-plane features
  (sills, arches, door, glass, lamps' side faces) are read directly. (Rim lip Ø 479 mm: the photo car
  most likely wears the optional 17-in Cup wheels; the model uses the standard 16-in.)
- **LINE side**: its drawn wheels are 1.8 % too close together (WB/L 0.526 vs 0.535; tyres drawn 5 %
  small), but its BODY matches corrected PHOTO01 within ±1 cm along the wings, roof and C-pillar when
  scaled on its overall length: nose 986.8 px, tail 311.4 px → 6.2852 mm/px both ways, heights from
  the drawn axle row 169.22 px = 0.316 m (roof 1.300 m). The front axle in this frame comes from
  wheel-plane features compared with PHOTO01 — door front shut line 0.958, door rear shut line (at
  0.60 m) 0.938, belt corner 0.942, handle 0.920 → **FA = 0.940 m** (photo perspective estimate 0.9397),
  RA = 3.212 m. `check-side.png` is calibrated on virtual wheel centres at the model's axles
  (837.24 / 475.76 px); the drawn tyres therefore sit 1.7 / 2.3 cm off the model wheels.
- **LINE plan**: nose 679.3 px, tail 0 px (own length → L; mirror at 1.69 m and B-pillar agree with the
  photo within 1–2 cm), centre row 367.5; lateral 1735 mm / widest outline (137.3 px, rear hips) =
  6.319 mm/px (the plan is 1 % narrow at its length scale; `ref-top.png` is resampled).
- **LINE front / rear**: centres 831.5 / 155.2 px, lateral 1735 mm / widest outline (136.7 / 142.3 px).
  These views are 1–5 % out of proportion (the rear view is 4 % wider than the front view); features
  visible in a side view take their heights from the side view, others use a uniform scale anchored
  on the roof (this reproduces the light-strip heights of the side view to 0.5 cm).
- Tyre check (scale-free): official Ø / WB = 0.632 / 2.272 = 0.278; LINE draws 0.265, VENDOR 0.289.

## Discrepancies and uncertainties

- Overall length and the axle position rest on the photo's perspective model (k from the tips, principal
  point at the image centre; ±1 cm per 140 px of principal-point shift) and on door features; both
  agree (0.940 / 0.9397 m). The 964 photo, measured the same way, gives a 964 FA of 0.886 m vs the 964
  drawing's 0.906 m (not changed).
- Front-lid centre-line profile is provisional (never visible in side views).
- LINE drawing errors found: wheels 1.8 % too close together, tyres 5 % small, windscreen line too far
  forward at its base, rear deck 1–4 cm too full, lamp box and arch lips drawn low; the rear view is
  4 % wider than the front view. The photo was used in each case.
- Rear lamp amber/red split: taken from the LINE rear-view divider (0.556 m), consistent with the
  European photos; PHOTO26 is a US car (all-red outer lamps, extra bumper guards — not modelled).
- The windscreen base: PHOTO01 shows black from 1.38 m (cowl panel + wipers), the plan's glass starts
  at 1.458 m; modelled as a black `cowl-grille` ahead of the glass.
- Body-colour door handle is shown as its recess outline. The spoiler is retracted (flush).
