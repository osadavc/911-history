## What differs from 05 (and why)

- **Rear wings +21 mm per side** (1610 → 1652 mm at the rear axle). sideZ =
  `plan_half_width(0.805, 0.826)`: the SC / Carrera 3.2 plan composite keeps its own rear-wing
  widening, scaled to the official 826 mm half-width. The shoulder (hip crest) is left as on
  05: the official figures only give the widest point, and the SC side views (1981 official
  drawing, SC 3-view) show the same arch cut-outs as the narrow car.
- **Wheels / tracks**: 185/70 & 215/60 VR 15 on 6J / 7J ATS cast wheels (`ats`: dished disc,
  five petal openings), silver face, polished edge (photo 31-detail-wheel), tracks 1369 / 1379.
- **MY1978 brightwork**: chrome window frames, drip rail, door handles and headlamp rings
  (geometry.json 07: "chrome … MY1978 only").
- **Mirror**: one electric flag mirror in body colour on the driver's door (museum SC
  08-front and the 1979 US brochure show one); size / position from photos 05-side-left
  (x 1.68, y 0.93) and 08-front (≈ 0.18 × 0.12 m head); outer edge 0.934 m from the centre
  line, printed on the official dd930 front view for the same door mirror.
- **Lid script "911SC"** just below the grille (photo 18-rear: 0.239 m wide, centre 0.795 m),
  `badge-model` slot in the top plane as on 03 / 05.
- No fog lamps (optional on the SC), no side repeaters (MY1981+), no spoilers.
- Single tailpipe on the car's left (photo 18-rear).

## References and calibration (checks)

- **Side**: `blueprints/07…/brochure_1981_911sc_us_techdata_side_dimension_drawing.png`, the
  official Porsche drawing re-issued for the SC (same drawing as the 1975 sheet used for 05).
  Axles = the 36.6 / 89.5 in extension lines x 255.65 / 633.15 → 6.0185 mm/px. The scan is
  squashed vertically; stretched by k = 1.0345 (official height; the rim ellipses measure
  55.8 × 58.4 px, i.e. 1.047). As on the 1975 sheet the rear overhang is drawn long
  (1.124 m vs printed 42.8 in = 1.087 m); `ref-side.png` compresses the part behind the rear
  arch by 0.950. The front overhang on this scan reads 0.907 m (printed 0.930) — scan
  distortion, not used.
- **Side photo**: `photos/07…/05-side-left.jpg` (1981 US SC, distant clean profile); hubs from
  circle fits of the polished 16-in rim lips (449.5, 838.5) / (1396.5, 841.5). Body sides,
  arches, sill strip, door shut line, bellows and handle line up; centre-line features (roof,
  bumper tips) project ~6 % larger (perspective, see 05). The tracing photo 01-side-left
  (black Fuchs on black tyres, exhibition hall) was checked too but its hub centres are
  less certain.
- **Plan**: vec-30946 (SC vendor plan): outline within ~1–2 % (the vendor plan is drawn
  slightly wide).
- **Front / rear**: SC 3-view `tbp-25361` end views, each calibrated on its own (front
  1610 mm over the wing outline x 24–297, roof 241 / ground 457; rear 1652 mm over x 438–714,
  roof 241 / ground 459) and stretched to isotropic copies (`ref-front.png`, `ref-rear.png`).
  Lamps, bumper band, overriders, lamp row and reflector band sit on the drawing.

## Known uncertainties

- The hip-crest z is unchanged from 05 (only the widest point is documented for the SC).
- MY1978 tracks: Excellence gives 1361 / 1367 mm for MY1978–79 (6J/7J), Porsche's 1981 data
  1369 / 1379 mm; specs.json's Porsche figure is used.
- RoW lamp layout (red / clear / amber) as 05; the US car in photo 18 has red indicators.
