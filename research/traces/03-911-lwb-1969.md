# 03-911-lwb-1969 — 911 T 2.0 Coupé, B-series (MY1969), long wheelbase

Script: `research/traces/03-911-lwb-1969.py` → `src/data/cars/03-911-lwb-1969.json`,
`research/traces/03-911-lwb-1969.calib.json`, checks in `research/traces/03-911-lwb-1969/`.

The stop is the car specs.json describes (coordinator): **1969 B-series 911 T**, 110 PS,
165 HR 15 on 5½J × 15, WB 2268, tracks 1362/1343, L 4163 / W 1610 / H 1320.

## Approach

The research is explicit that the LWB change was mechanical: "the rear wheels moved back
57 mm on longer semi-trailing arms **without lengthening the body**", "the wheel openings
were adapted", "subtle flare", "indicators a bit bigger" (history.md, geometry.json 03). So:

* **Shell** (doors, glass, roof, lids, bumpers, plan) = the same pressing as stop 02, traced
  from the same 1967 factory body drawing with the 02 calibration (2.4567 mm/px, ground
  y 644.3). The nose, the front-lid line and the plan nose were re-read here; the rest are
  02's readings of that sheet (verified on an overlay).
* **LWB changes** from the LWB references:
  * rear axle at FA + 2.268 m; the rear wheel opening moved back with it by 57 mm
    (23.2 px). Verified on the Autocar LWB side view: its rear arch is centred on its
    (LWB) rear hub (arch centre 907.5 px vs hub 905.9 px) and ends ~5 cm ahead of the rear
    bumper (model: 3.9 cm); the 1973 T profile shows the arch reaching the bumper.
  * body length unchanged: Autocar LWB sheet, rear tip 3.262 m behind its front hub vs
    3.245 m on the factory sheet — same body.
  * flares: the Autocar draws a double arch line (~5 cm lip band) where the 1967 drawing has
    one; photos 01/22 show the rolled lip. Modelled by taking the arch edge (rockerZ) 7 mm
    further out than 02 (5 mm inside the plan width instead of 12 mm; flush would bulge the
    loft past the unchanged 1610 mm width). Flare size is not published.
* **Front tip**: no front bumper guards on the 1969 T (photo 22, the July-1969 911 T 2.0;
  geometry.json 03 "omit on the model"), so x = 0 is the bumper face at the centre line
  (factory side view 1712 px, plan row scan 1714). Rear guards are fitted (photos 04, 08,
  11, 23, 24; geometry.json rear bumper) → rear tip = guard tip as in 02.

## Parts and decals (sources)

* Wheels: 165 HR 15 → **0.60 m** (same tyre as 02, measured there at 0.265–0.2675 × WB).
  **Fuchs** 5-spoke: every LWB 911 T in the research photos (22 = July 1969 T, 01, 08, 09–11,
  16, 23) and both LWB drawings show Fuchs; polished spokes on a black star (geometry.json).
  The 1969 T's *standard* wheel design is not documented in the research (see open doubts).
* Tracks 1.362 / 1.343 (specs.json).
* Bumpers: body colour; black rubber profile strip with bright edges (geometry.json 03,
  photos) at 0.423–0.463 m (Autocar rear/front bumper columns; photo 01: strip at 0.75–0.88
  of the blade height, Autocar 0.70–0.88). Side ids `bumper-front/-rear` (rubber),
  front/rear `bumper-*-face` (bright frame) + `bumper-insert-*` (rubber) — same ids as 01/04.
* Front lamps: horn grille (chrome) | clear | amber outboard (photo 22), lateral split as the
  1963 elevation, heights = factory housing (0.507–0.586 m). Side view: the amber wrap-round
  0.45–0.62 m ahead of the front axle (photo 01, near the wheel plane) — the "bigger" B-series
  unit. No guards, no fog lamps (photo 22).
* Rear lamps: sit on the bumper (0.516–0.613 m; photo 01 offsets from the bumper top);
  split from the straight-on 911 E rear photo 04 as fractions of the body half-width at lamp
  height (0.66 m): red 0.45–0.53, clear 0.53–0.56, amber 0.56–0.63; side wrap-round 0.66–0.85 m
  behind the rear axle (photo 01).
* Rear: guards (chrome + black rubber face), red reflectors on the bumper below the lamps
  (photos 04/23/24), plate, gold "PORSCHE" (geometry.json: gold to MY1972) and "911 T"
  script under the grille, grille 0.44 m half-width (factory 0.43 / Autocar 0.45) at the
  factory x (Autocar LWB plan agrees within 2 cm).
* Door handle with push button (concealed lever only from the C series, MY1970); chrome sill
  strip (photo 22) between the arches; vent-window bar; single round chrome mirror on the
  driver's door; round filler flap on the left front wing (factory plan oval).
* Exhaust: single pipe below the bumper on the car's **left** (geometry.json; photo 04).
* Headlamp: same values as 02 (unchanged part).

## Known uncertainties / open doubts

* **Standard wheel of the 1969 T**: specs.json gives only 5½J × 15. All research photos and
  both LWB drawings show Fuchs, so Fuchs is modelled; if the T had steel wheels as standard,
  swap `design` to `steel-hubcap` (research does not say).
* **Arch height / ride height** (applies to 02 as well): the Autocar LWB sheet and the
  1965 studio photo put the arch tops at ~0.62–0.66 m, the 02 calibration of the factory
  sheet at 0.72 m (tyre gap 0.12 m vs 0.06–0.08 m in the photos). The factory sheet itself
  carries a horizontal "Plan 0" datum (y ≈ 487 px) with a vertical "330" dimension down to
  a dashed ground-like line (y ≈ 622) and "51" from Plan 0 down to the wheel-centre mark —
  i.e. hub 0.279 m above that line and a roof of 1.265 m: a loaded design position. The 02
  trace instead uses the unladen 1320 mm height with the wheel at its radius. 03 follows
  02 for a clean morph; if 02 is re-calibrated, re-run this script (it copies 02's datum).
* Front-lamp and tail-lamp sizes of the B-series ("a bit bigger") are measured on photos of
  later LWB cars (1973 T, 911 E), not on a 1969 car.
* Rear guards, reflectors and rubber faces vary by market/year; modelled per the LWB photos.
* 02 uses slightly different values for a few shared parts (fuel-flap position/size, lid
  line at the nose, cowl slot width); this trace uses its own readings, so those small
  parts shift a little in the 02→03 morph.
