## Calibration / placement

- Shell = stop 17's (vendor 992.1, hub-calibrated) at official 992.2 figures: ride height +4 mm
  (1,302 vs 1,298), each overhang +11.5 mm (4,519 → 4,542). The split of the 23 mm is not published;
  fitting the camera (distance, principal point) and the overhang split to the profile photos P04 and
  P01 is degenerate (the principal point of a cropped press photo moves a photo overhang by 1–3 cm
  per 100 px), so the equal split is kept. Result: L 4.542, FA 1.0455, RA 3.4955, H 1.302 m.
- New aprons in plan: the vendor 992.2 plan vs the vendor 992.1 plan, each normalised to its own
  half-width 0.45 m behind the tip, shows both ends narrower near the tips (more rounded corners
  around the larger intakes): nose 0.88–0.96, tail 0.85–0.97 of the 992.1's within 0.3 m of the tips.
  Smoothed factors (`NOSE_F`, `TAIL_F`) scale every z curve there. This also agrees with the profile
  photos showing a shorter-looking nose than the 992.1 model would give.
- Front heights: vendor 992.2 front view (roof y 80 = 1.302 m, 5.936 mm/px; its lamp centre 0.708 m
  matches the 992.1's 0.704), checked on P17 (EU plate 110 mm = 62 px) and P15.
- Rear heights: P19 plate 922–1175 px = 520 mm → 2.055 mm/px, relative to the red strip line (v 467)
  at the 992.1 bar height 0.772 m + 4 mm (both vendor drawings put the bar at 0.77 m; the photos
  scatter ±2.5 cm).

## Checks

- `check-side.png` (vendor 992.2 upper side view, hub-calibrated): profile, glasshouse and arches sit on
  the drawing; its tail extends ~2 % further (the drawing's own length error).
- `check-top.png`: the narrowed nose and tail sit on the vendor 992.2 plan outline; glass and grille on
  the drawn ones.
- `check-front.png` / `check-rear.png`: intakes, lamps, strip, reflectors and the black sections on the
  drawing; the plate boxes sit where the drawing shows the plate panel.
- `check-photo-side.png`: pinhole overlay on the official Carrera profile P04 (rim-lip fits
  1248.91/1011.71, 597.28/1005.66 px; camera 14 m, principal point at mid-wheelbase / hub height):
  roof, tail, glasshouse, door, flap and strip on the photo; the drawn nose projects ~30–40 px ahead of
  the photo's at this camera distance (the fit needs ~8 m, which the roof does not support) — the
  photo's camera geometry is uncertain, see the placement note.

## Uncertainties

- Everything not listed above is the 992.1 trace; the 992.2's aprons are represented by the plan
  factors and decals, not by a traced 992.2 side profile (no reliable drawing).
- Overhang split of the +23 mm (equal) and the light-strip height (±2.5 cm).
- The 992.2 front plate / sensor panel layout is from P17 (EU street car) and P15 (US, no plate).
