## Calibration (each view on its own)

The sheet's views are ~2.5 % short in length relative to their heights/widths, so the axes
are calibrated separately against the official figures.

- **Side** (car's left, nose left): axle tick lines x = 328.0 / 989.0 px → 661 px = 2350 mm
  (3.555 mm/px). Bumper extremes 48.5 / 1282.0 px (front plate excluded) give 4386 mm
  (−1.0 %); the missing 44 mm are spread over both overhangs (×1.022) so L and WB both
  hold. Vertical: tyre contact 942, roof 566 → 1305 mm (3.471 mm/px). Tyre outline radius
  92 px → Ø 0.639 m vs 0.637 m computed from 205/50 R17 (tyre/WB 0.271).
- **Plan**: same x mapping; centre line fitted per column (tilted 2 px); 507 px = 1765 mm.
- **Front**: centre 418.5, body 165.5–672.5 px = 1765 mm; ground 1321, roof 954.
- **Rear**: centre 953, 508 px = 1765 mm; vertical through ground 1326, lamp-bottom line
  1134 (0.659 m from the side view) and roof 957.
- Result: length 4.430, lofted width 1.766, height 1.305, overhangs 1.016 / 1.064 m.

## How the body was built

- `topY` = side silhouette (auto), except the front lid centre: from the lid's front
  edge (front view y 1150 px → 0.608 m, = side silhouette there) rising to the cowl
  (0.881 m), kept under the lamp/wing silhouette.
- `rockerY` = side lower edge: hand-read nose/sill/tail + both arch lips traced radially
  around the hubs (outer fender-lip line 100–124 px from the hub centre, median-smoothed).
- `sideZ` = plan half-width (auto, mirrors bridged), then compensated on a dense grid for
  the Catmull-Rom bulge (max residual 4 mm except the first 4 cm of the nose).
- **Headlamp plane**: three points identified in all three views (inner-front corner,
  outer-front corner, top of the round lens) give pitch 64.2°, yaw 14.0°. The front-view
  outline is lifted onto that plane and stored in lens-plane coordinates (heights ÷ cos
  pitch), so the rendered lamp projects back onto the traced outline (`check-lamp.png`:
  side / plan / front, placement replicates `placeLamp`'s raycast; it lands 1.8 cm above
  the traced plane centre).
- **Wing top** in the lamp zone: the renderer seats the flat lens on the outermost body hit
  of nine rays (lens centre + a circle of radius max|outline| + ring), so the wing must not
  rise above the lens plane there. `11-996-1-1998.lampseat.py` puts P4 (belt) on the
  lamp's inner edge and P3 (crest) on its outer edge, both 4 mm under the lens plane, over
  the central 70 % of the lamp's length, and keeps the lid centre ≤ inner edge + 12 mm
  (996: lid lower than the wings, photo 18). Outside the lamp the crest follows the wing
  shoulder line (plan feature line z 0.74→0.82 m; side highlight y 0.71→0.80 m), capped
  12 mm inside the plan outline. Replicated placement: lens pushed out 11 mm (was 35 mm).
- Nose/tail: the lower bumper faces curve under; P1 stays wide (flat bumper bottoms,
  photos 07/10) and the bumper faces are repainted body colour by `bumper-front-face` /
  `bumper-rear-face` so only the black lip (front) and dark diffuser area (rear) remain
  in the underbody colour.

## Known uncertainties

- The drawing is of a Carrera 4 with sunroof (sunroof not drawn, not modelled).
- Lid centre height inside the lamp zone is inferred (hidden in the side view); the
  lamps' round tops stand ~2–4 cm above it as the silhouette requires.
- Windscreen: drawing gives 28° from horizontal on the centre line and 37° along the
  A-pillar edge; the published "55°" is ambiguous (reference axis not stated).
- Loft limits: the section closes to a line at the tip, which shows as a faint vertical
  shading crease on the nose; the fried-egg lamp is a flat plane (real lens is curved).
- Rear lamp colours: MY1998 amber indicators per geometry.json and the press car;
  later 996.1 cars have smoked/clear indicator lenses.
- Photo labelling slip: `photos/11…/02-side-right.jpg` shows the car's left side.
