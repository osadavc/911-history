# 06-930-turbo-1975 — 911 Turbo 3.0 Coupé (type 930), MY1975, whale tail

`05-g-series-1974.py` and applies the documented Turbo differences (flared wings, deep front
spoiler, whale tail, trim, wheels).

## Differences from the G-series shell, each measured

- **Plan (sideZ)**: the dd930 plan outline (mean of both halves; mirror and dimension-line
  regions excluded) scaled to 850 / 887.5 mm at the axles; front flare from x ≈ 0.2 to
  1.45 m (max 0.857 m at 0.96 m before scaling), rear flare from 2.7 to 3.69 m (tail end read
  on a 10 px grid: 0.884 / 0.855 / 0.819 / 0.764 m at x 3.32 / 3.54 / 3.61 / 3.69); nose and
  tail from the G-series composite (same bumpers).
- **Rear arch lip** scaled 1.04 about the rear wheel centre (dd930 arch top 0.361 m above
  the axle vs 0.346 m on the narrow-body drawing); front arch unchanged (0.37–0.386 vs 0.373).
- **Front spoiler**: vertical front face at x 0.13 m (dd930 x 237 px), lower edge 0.20 m
  (dd930 0.19–0.21 m, Polish plan 0.205 m; the 14.5° approach angle printed on the dd930
  gives 0.197 m for that face position) back to x 0.50, then into the arch leg. Rendered
  matt black: the forward face is swept by the underbody segment (P1 is not pulled in over
  the drop, `apron=False`), plus `spoiler-front` (side) / `spoiler-front-face` (front) decals.
- **Whale tail** (`wing`, kind `whale`, extruded side profile): x0 3.56 m (where the lid
  centre reaches the wing top), x1 = L − 0.066 = 4.225 m (tbp-77242: trailing edge 11 px ahead
  of the tail; Polish plan 0.071 m); top y 0.967 at the leading edge (tbp-77242 (600, 67))
  rising 0.018 to 0.985 at the trailing edge (tbp-77242 0.985, Polish 0.983); thickness
  0.065 (trailing-edge thickness: tbp-77242 0.072, Polish 0.058); black rubber lip 0.025 m
  (drawings ≈ 0.015, press photo ≈ 0.04 curl-up); half-span 0.64 m (tbp-77242 rear view, wing
  x 848–1062 px at 5.956 mm/px).
- **Trim**: anodised-black window frames, drip rail and handles (rendered with the rubber
  finish because satin *line* decals are drawn as panel gaps), body-colour headlamp rings
  and body-colour flag mirror on the driver's door only (passenger mirror standard from
  MY1988); mirror outer edge 0.934 m from the centre line (printed on the dd930 front view).
- **Fog lamps** in the spoiler below the bumper: photo 08-front (headlamp spacing 859 px =
  1.256 m; depth-corrected) → z 0.40–0.56 m, y 0.28–0.36 m (dd930: z 0.38–0.55, y 0.31–0.39).
- **Twin tailpipes** on the car's left at z 0.59 / 0.66 (tbp-77242 rear view), outlet height
  as the G-series pipe. **"turbo" script** centred on the lid below the wing (`badge-model`,
  top plane, as the other G-series stops). Black Fuchs star with polished lip (photo 01),
  tyres 0.640 / 0.639 m.

## Known uncertainties / not representable

- The wing is a constant-thickness extruded profile: the neck that joins its underside to
  the lid, the drooping outer ends and the intake grille in its top are not modelled (the
  slab's front is buried in the lid, so it reads as a plate standing off the deck).
- Height 1320 (Excellence, 3.0) vs 1310 mm (Porsche, 3.3); the 1320 body of 05/07 is used.
- MY1975 tyre sizes: Porsche 1976 US data (185/70 & 215/60 VR 15) vs Excellence
  (205/50 & 225/50 VR 15); specs.json's MY1975 standard sizes used.
- Spoiler depth: drawings 0.20 m, press photo ≈ 0.15 m.
- Stone-chip foils (MY1977+), the tea tray (MY1978+) and side repeaters are not part of the
  MY1975 car.
