## Parts

* Tyres 165 × 15 have no aspect ratio → measured: No. 57 side photo, tyre 256 px over a
  951 px hub-to-hub wheelbase (rim-fit centres) = 0.269 × WB → 0.593 m → **0.60 m** (as 02).
  Rim circle Ø 177 px = 0.411 m confirms the scale (15-in flange). Steel wheel + chrome cap.
* Headlamp: front sketch centres z 0.611, y 0.670; lens Ø 0.195, chrome ring to Ø 0.238;
  side sketch ring face inclined atan(66/148) = **24°** (the 1967 factory side view gives
  23°) → pitch 24, yaw 4.
* Mirror: single round chrome mirror on the left door (geometry.json): front sketch head
  Ø 0.079 m centred z 0.796, y 0.836; side sketch x 1440 px.
* Exhaust: one tailpipe on the left (−z) below the bumper, sketch (2905–2975, 1565–1595),
  z 0.55 from the No. 57 rear photo.

rear: taillight / reverse-light / indicator-rear, bumper-rear-face, bumper-insert-rear,

(`side: "left"`), hood-gap, lid-gap.

Deliberately absent (show car): guards, fog lamps, sill trim (history.md + 'TYP 901'
photos), rear badge (undocumented for the stand car — geometry.json open question).

**Slot-id note:** 02 (SWB example) uses `bumper-strip-front/rear` for its *side-view*
chrome strip and again (duplicate ids) for its front/rear-plane strips, while TRACING.md
defines `bumper-strip-*` as front/rear-plane rubber strips (and 09/11/15 follow the table).
To never pair a side decal with a front decal, this car uses the table ids
`bumper-front/-rear` + `bumper-front-face/-rear-face` and 02's `bumper-insert-*` for the
rubber insert (constants at the top of the decal section, trivial to rename).

## Known uncertainties

* No 901 plan or rear drawing: plan widths and rear-face positions come from the 1967
  production drawing / 1964 No. 57 photos (sources.json: body near-identical).
* Bumper height: the 1963 sketch puts the strip 0.075–0.116 m above the hub; No. 57 (1964)
  shows 0.053–0.087 m — the sketch is used for the show car (±2.5 cm).
* The IAA car reportedly had twin tailpipes (history.md) but only one is drawn anywhere;
  one is modelled. Rear plate position follows No. 57.
* Rear-lamp colour split (red / clear / amber inboard→outboard) is from No. 57; 02 has the
  front lamp colours the other way round (amber inboard) — photos 02-front/06 show amber
  outboard.
* Lamp pitch 24° here vs 15° in 02 (both drawings read 23–24°).
