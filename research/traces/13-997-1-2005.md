## Calibration

- **Cutaway** (nose right): hubs x 834.0 / 266.5 → 567.5 px = 2350 mm (4.141 mm/px); body
  ends 1073 / 7 → 4414 mm (−0.3 %), overhangs ×1.006 to 4427. Ground y 331, roof y 12 →
  1310 mm (4.107 mm/px). Circles of the official tyre sizes sit on its tyres (`ref-cutaway-cal.png`).
  Its line work is ~5 px thick: the silhouette is eroded 2 px to the line centres.
- **vec-1030**: plan tips 34/803 px (5.757 mm/px), centre row 629, half-width 156 px = 904 mm;
  front view centre 1055, 151 px = 904 mm, ground 330 / roof 100; rear view centre 1053,
  152 px, ground 748 / roof 521; side view (for the ends) tips 34/802, ground 330.5, 5.683 mm/px.
  Its wheels are drawn ~5 cm too far back (overhangs 1.05/1.05 m vs 0.99/1.08 m from the
  cutaway), so it is only used tip-relative in x.
- **Photo 02**: hub centres (1467.0, 713.3)/(464.6, 691.3) from rim-edge circle fits,
  426.7 px/m, loaded hub height 0.313 m; all features used lie within a few cm of the near
  wheel plane where this flat calibration is exact.
- **Photo 07**: hubs (1267.0, 800.5)/(636.4, 800.0), 268.3 px/m, loaded hub height 0.317 m.
- Result: L 4.427, WB 2.350, overhangs 0.996 / 1.081 m, width 1.808, roof 1.310.

## Decisions (where sources disagreed)

- **Overhang split**: cutaway (0.99/1.08) over vec-1030 (1.05/1.05); vec-1030's WB/L error
  is recorded in its sources.json; the photos put the arches where the cutaway does.
- **Front end — which source sees what**: near the nose the three sources disagree (at
  x 0.5: cutaway 0.768, vec-1030 0.753, museum photo 07 0.729, photo 02 0.705 m). Photo 02 is a
  close shot (camera fitted to the loft: D ≈ 5 m, height 1.0 m): its silhouette there is the
  near-side lamp/wing, while the higher lid centre (z = 0, 0.74 m further away) is hidden by
  perspective; photo 07 is further away and lies in between; the two orthographic drawings
  agree within 1.5 cm. So: `topY` (lid centre) = cutaway silhouette from the lid's front edge
  (front view 0.623 m) to the cowl; front-wing crest behind the lamp (z ≈ 0.70) and the nose
  corner in front of it (z ≈ 0.72) = photo 02 silhouette back-projected through the fitted
  camera at their own depth (0.775 m at x 0.62 → 0.834 at 1.0 → 0.87 at 1.36); photo 16
  (front 3/4) confirms the domed lid standing above the lamps.
- **Headlamp**: photo 02 sliver back-projected at the lamp's depth (lower-front tip x 0.361,
  upper-rear tip x 0.607 — 2.5 cm ahead of the flat mapping) with the vec-1030 front-view
  outline (0.23 × 0.22 m, y 0.575–0.792) → pitch 48.6°, yaw 5°, centre x 0.484 m. The wing is
  seated on the lens plane over the lamp's whole length (+1.5 cm), P4 extended 5.6 cm inboard
  so the renderer's probe circle (r 0.173 m) also lands on the plane; the domed lid is left
  alone. Replicated placement push: 9 mm (it was 151 mm with the cutaway nose).
- **Lower body**: the cutaway's lowest lines are the underbody (0.12 m); the paint edge (sill
  0.195 m, arches, nose) comes from photo 02; the black front lip / chin (vec-1030) and a
  black rear diffuser down to 0.165 m (photo 11) are the `floorY`, painted dark by the renderer.
- **Rear-window base** (x 3.15–3.85): mean of vec-1030 and the 996.1 roof (they agree within
  1.6 cm; the cutaway, which draws the glass as a thick band, sits 2–3.5 cm lower).
- **Rear deck**: lid-edge / rear-window-edge drops below the centre line carried over from
  the 996.1 trace (same roof, near-flat deck); rear hips from photo 02 (tail-lamp top 0.79 m).
- **Tail**: the rear face is near-vertical from 0.38 to 0.62 m in both drawings; the section at
  x = L spans that face (floor 0.40 → top 0.62) so the plate area stays rear-facing.
- **Tailpipe**: z 0.55 m, centre 0.23 m (photo 11 + vec-1030 rear view), one per side.

## Known uncertainties

- Front-wing crest z (≈ 0.70 m behind the lamp) is inferred from the lamp top and the filler
  flap's position/orientation; no source shows it directly.
- The nose paint edge is photo-measured relative to the tip; ±2 cm.
- Headlamp lens is a flat plane (the real clear cover is domed); pitch from a small side sliver.
- US vs EU bumpers: photos 02/08/11 show a US car (4461 mm); the body uses the EU length 4427
  from the drawings (US-only side markers are kept as documented).
