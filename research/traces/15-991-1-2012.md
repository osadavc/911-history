## Calibration

- **Official side view (OS)**: axle extension lines of the printed "2.450 mm" at x 504.5/1215.5 px
  → 3.446 mm/px; both drawn wheels are centred on them (tyre 97 px radius = 334 mm vs 335 mm
  for 235/40 R19). Length extension lines 216.5/1521.5 px → 4,496.8 mm (printed 4,499, −0.05 %).
  Ground = hub + 335.3 mm (y 1189.06; drawn ground line top edge 1189). Roof stroke centre
  y 812 → 1,299 mm (printed 1,294–1,298). All readings are stroke centres.
- **Official front/rear views (OF/OR)**: centre lines 369 / 1357.5, ground 470; across 1,808 mm over
  the body sides (536 px) = 3.373 mm/px; up: roof stroke centre (y 83) = the side view's 1,299 mm →
  3.357 mm/px (0.5 % from square). The printed track lines sit ~25 mm inboard of the official
  tracks and are not used.
- **Vendor views**: plan tips 49/818 px (4,491 mm), centre row 629.5; the vendor plan is 2 % wider
  than the official width, so plan half-widths are scaled to 1,808 mm (5.74 mm/px across). The
  vendor plan's glasshouse is ~10 cm longer than both side views show (drawn separately), so only
  its widths are used. Front/rear views: roof edge 107/525, tyre bottoms 330/747, 313 px = 1,808 mm
  → 5.79 / 5.80 mm/px; +8 mm (the vendor Carrera S is 1,293–1,295 mm tall vs the Carrera's 1,303).
- **991.1 vs the 991.2 drawing**: ride height +4 mm (1,303 mm official vs 1,299 measured; the
  991.2 got −10 mm PASM ride height); length 4,491 vs 4,496.8 → each overhang −3 mm
  (`remap_overhangs`; the split is not published, 4,491 − 2,450 = 2,041 mm total).
- Result: L 4.491, WB 2.450 (FA 0.989, RA 3.439), lofted height 1.3035 m, lofted width 1.818 m
  (0.5 % over; residual Catmull-Rom bulge at the front wheel arch).

## Body curves

- `topY`: the side silhouette (auto-traced, wipers bridged) — nose, lid, cowl (0.91 m, front
  view 0.910), screen, roof, rear window, lid, tail. Only at the lamp's top-rear corner (x 0.55–0.8)
  is the silhouette the fender; there the lid centre is taken 12 mm lower.
- `crestY/Z`: fender top along the lens centre line, then the silhouette to x 1.05 m, then the
  crease that runs into the door top (0.870 m; front view fender peak 0.866–0.878 at z 0.64–0.68);
  door shoulder 15 mm under it; rear hip crest 0.879 m at z 0.78–0.79 (rear view).
- `beltY/Z`: front-lid shut line (2 cm under the lid centre, z from the VT lid line); cabin = glass
  base (0.93 m, z 0.737); behind it the foot of the raised engine-lid deck (z 0.53–0.56).
- `roofY/Z`: A-pillar rear edge (front edge of the glass frame), then the top of the side-glass
  opening (1.215 m, z 0.60 — VT glass strip, front/rear greenhouse outline), blending over the rear
  quarter onto the rear-window side edge (z 0.49) and the lid deck's top edge. Using the glass top
  (per TRACING.md) removes the 1 cm roof bulge the loft made with the roof-panel edge.
- `sideZ`: VT plan outline (mirrors bridged) scaled to 1,808 mm, then pulled in where the loft
  bulges past P2 so the lofted width matches the plan (`correct_overshoots`); `roofY` is lowered the
  same way at the cowl and lid deck where the loft rose above the traced silhouette.
- `rockerY`: traced lower edge, arches = outer edge of the drawn wheel-well band (ray-cast from the
  hubs); `sideY` kept 3.5 cm above the arch edge so no skirt hangs over the tyres.

- Wheels: 235/40 ZR 19 → Ø 0.671 m, 285/35 ZR 19 → Ø 0.682 m, rim 0.4826; photos 01/04/06 show ten

## Uncertainties

- The shell is the 991.2 drawing; the 991.1 is assumed identical apart from ride height and the
  8 mm length split. Apron/tail parts come from the vendor drawing (±1 cm) and photos.
- Headlight pitch/yaw and the lens outline are fitted to three views, ±3°.
- Plan widths depend on the vendor plan (scaled −2 %); roof-rail z ±2 cm.
- Slot ids not in TRACING.md's table, used consistently for stops 15–18 and matching stop 13 where
  it has them: `intake-front-centre`, `reflector-rear`, `indicator-side`, `taillight-side`, `crest`
  (16 adds `vent-rear`).
- The nose closes to a short line at 0.25–0.27 m (the 991's foremost point is the lower lip);
  `sideY` there is kept between floor and top so the first sections do not fold.
