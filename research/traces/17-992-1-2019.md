## Calibration

- **Side view (VS)**: hub centres from circle fits to the rim lips (F 229.35/272.30, R 648.49/269.05
  px; rms 0.7/2.3 px) → 419.14 px = 2,450 mm → 5.845 mm/px. Nose 52 / tail 826 px → 4,524 mm (+0.1 %
  vs 4,519; each overhang trimmed 2.7 mm). Roof stroke centre 109.5 px; ground set from the official
  1,298 mm height (y 331.6) — the drawn C4S wheels (on 20/21-inch tyres) then stand ~1.5 px higher.
- **Plan (VT)**: tips 50.5/826.5 px mapped onto the side view's tips; widest 320 px = 1,852 mm
  (5.79 mm/px across); centre row 628. Per column the narrower half-width is used and a running
  median removes watermark spikes; mirrors bridged.
- **Front / rear views**: centres 1053.75 / 1054.25, body sides 1,852 mm over 319.5 / 320.5 px, ground
  from the official height.
- **Photos**: P03 hubs by rim-lip circle fits (1321.87/974.17, 543.79/979.45 px) → hub similarity
  (3.149 mm/px, roll −0.82°): light-bar red line 0.774 m, housing 0.752–0.789 m; the vendor rear view
  gives 0.769 m, so the bar is placed at 0.772 m. P14 (straight rear of a base Carrera): EU plate
  826.7–1033.9 px = 520 mm → 2.512 mm/px, centre u 930.3, heights relative to the bar line (v 557.8).
  (P01 and the 992.2 photos scatter ±2.5 cm around this from perspective and camera pitch.)
- Result: L 4.519, FA 1.034, RA 3.484, lofted height 1.298 m, lofted width 1.842 m (−0.5 %).

(gap band), windscreen / rear window / lid lines / crest in plan, fuel flap (right side only), apron
strips (front + side wrap), three-slat intakes, central opening, plate, black lip; light bar

between the tailpipes (0.293–0.405 m), reflectors, black lower rear (to 0.437 m), black grille field
(the body's decal stripes only run across the car, so the lengthwise louvres cannot be striped).

edge), from the drawing's outline. Wheels 0.671 / 0.7145 m (= Porsche's 671/715 mm), rims 19/20 in, `twin-spoke` (standard

pitch 30°, yaw 10°. Mirrors: rectangular door-top housings (shape `aero`). One oval tailpipe each side
(P14: z ±0.426 m, 0.316 m).
