## Approach

The 997.2 is a facelift of the 997.1 (geometry.json 14; coordinator brief: derive it from the
997.1 trace plus the documented differences — the free 997.2 drawings are a vendor side+top
(vec-838, same ~5 cm wheel-position error as the 997.1 vendor views) and a Sport Classic 4-view
(wide body, ducktail, coarse). So the body is the 997.1 trace, and each documented change is
measured on the 997.2 photos **against the matching 997.1 photo with the same method**, so the
methods' shared errors cancel.

## Known uncertainties

- Apron surface changes (deeper intakes, the diffuser shape) are only decals; the body shape is
  the 997.1's.
- The +8 mm length split is assumed equal front/rear.
- The rear mapping assumes the lamps' inner ends and the lid are unchanged (visually identical in
  photos 11/12); its vertical scale follows the vec-1030 lamp (15 % taller than in photo 11).
- The DRL strip is emissive white (daytime running lights on), as in the press/brochure photos.
